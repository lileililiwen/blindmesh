## The limits of the evidence

- The reason of a case and the note of a request of more evidence are part of the answer of `GET /v1/disputes/{id}` and the page of the case shows both. The parties of the case and the moderation staff read them, and an unrelated account reads neither the case nor its reason.
- The command that opens a case and the command that bans an account have no page of their own. The journeys send them through the API with the session of the browser, and the client library carries both.
- The browser journeys ran with the Chromium browser only, because the Firefox and the WebKit runtimes are not installed on this machine.
