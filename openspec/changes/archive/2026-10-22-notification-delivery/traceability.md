## The scenarios of the contract and the test that observes them

| Scenario | Test | What the test observes |
|---|---|---|
| NOTIF-1 success | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF1DeliveryTests.cs` — `Notif1Success_OneInAppItemAppearsAndAnEligibleEmailIsSentWithAStableMessageId` | One in-app item of the contributor in the list of the API, one email in the real mail sink of the test run, the identifier of the message is the identifier of the delivery, the body holds the generic summary and the relative deep link and no private title, no finding text and no absolute address |
| NOTIF-1 rejection | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF1DeliveryTests.cs` — `Notif1Rejection_AFailedAttemptKeepsItsRetryStateAndStatesTheUncertainty` | The delivery stays failed with the counted attempt, a redacted reason without host, port or credential, the next attempt in the future, the identifier of the message unchanged, and a log line that states the uncertainty and never claims that the email arrives exactly once |
| NOTIF-1 boundary | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF1DeliveryTests.cs` — `Notif1Boundary_TheDeliveryIsDeadLetteredWithARedactedOperatorAlertWhileOtherDeliveriesContinue` | The wait of every attempt is the delay of the schedule of the product, the delivery dies after its last attempt with a redacted reason, the operator of the product holds the alert in the product and the log names the dead letter, and the other deliveries go out |
| NOTIF-2 success | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF2PreferencesTests.cs` — `Notif2Success_TheUnreadStateAndThePreferencesPersistWithTheFeedbackOfTheServer` | The item of the list is read after the command, the read state persists, the preferences are answered with the committed state and the preferences persist |
| NOTIF-2 rejection | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF2PreferencesTests.cs` — `Notif2Rejection_AnotherAccountGetsNoSummaryAndNoActorIdentity` | The list of another account holds no item of the first account, the command of another account is refused with 404 and the answer holds neither the summary of the item nor the identity of the caller, and the item of the first account stays unread |
| NOTIF-2 boundary | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Notifications/NOTIF2PreferencesTests.cs` — `Notif2Boundary_AQueuedEmailOfARevokedMembershipHoldsNoPrivateContent` | The membership is revoked before the message goes out, the message is reduced to a safe generic account notice and holds no private content, no deep link to the source and no absolute address |
| NOTIF-UX complete journey | `tests/e2e/notification-delivery/journey.spec.ts` — `NOTIF-UX the notification of an accepted work appears and is marked as read`, `NOTIF-UX the preferences of the notifications are saved and announced by the server` | The item of the list appears, the marker of the unread items disappears after the command and stays away after a reload, the saved state of the preferences is announced only after the server confirmed it and the state of the server is the state of the page |
| NOTIF-UX recover from failure | `tests/e2e/notification-delivery/journey.spec.ts` — `NOTIF-UX an unavailable API explains the failure and offers a retry`, `NOTIF-UX the list of a contributor without a notification explains the next action` | The failure of the API is explained and a retry reads the state again, the empty list explains the next action |
| NOTIF-UX responsive keyboard operation | `tests/e2e/notification-delivery/journey.spec.ts` — `NOTIF-UX the pages of the notifications have no horizontal overflow at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels`, `NOTIF-UX the journey of the notifications works with the keyboard only`, `NOTIF-UX the journey of the notifications works in the dark theme`; `tests/e2e/notification-delivery/accessibility.a11y.ts` — the four runs of axe | No horizontal scrolling at the three widths, the next action is reached and opened with the keyboard, the pages hold no violation of axe in both themes |
