## The scenarios of the contract and the test that observes them

| Scenario | Test | What the test observes |
|---|---|---|
| OPS-1 success | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS1RecoveryTests.cs` — `OPS1SuccessTheOperatorRestoresTheBackupIntoAnIsolatedDatabaseAndEveryCheckPasses` | The rehearsal dumps the real PostgreSQL of the test run, restores the dump into an isolated database of its own, the four verdicts pass, the isolated database is dropped again and the rehearsal is listed for the operator |
| OPS-1 rejection | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS1RecoveryTests.cs` — `OPS1RejectionAFailedLedgerCheckBlocksThePromotionAndTheRecoveryPathStaysAvailable` | A transaction of the journal that does not sum to zero makes the ledger check fail, the rehearsal has the status `failed`, the gate blocks the promotion and names the command of the check, and the runbook of the backup and the restore still holds the recovery path |
| OPS-1 boundary | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS1RecoveryTests.cs` — `OPS1BoundaryTheDeletionRequestRemovesTheIdentityAndKeepsTheProvenanceOfTheSettledRecords` | The email, the display name, the biography, the skills and the languages of the subject are cleared, the account keeps a pseudonym and is closed, the provider identity is gone, the sessions are revoked, and the rows of the ledger keep the pseudonym and never the identity |
| OPS-2 success | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS2PilotGateTests.cs` — `OPS2SuccessTheGateReportsTheMeasuredCountsAndTheRecordedEvidenceOfThePilot` | Every count of the gate is the count of the database, every item names the command that produced its value, and the numbers the operator recorded are reported as they are |
| OPS-2 rejection | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS2PilotGateTests.cs` — `OPS2RejectionTheGateIsRefusedAndTheReleaseStaysBlockedWithTheFailedCommand` | A visitor is refused with 401, an ordinary account and a moderator are refused with 403, the gate of the administrator stays blocked while the restore evidence is missing and every blocked item names its command |
| OPS-2 boundary | `tests/Blindmesh.Tests/Operations/OPS2PilotGateTests.cs` — `OPS2BoundaryTheLatencyAndTheAvailabilityStayTargetsUntilTheirWindowIsDocumented` | The p95 of the latency and the availability are reported as `target, not yet measured`, they never count as passed and their measured value never holds the target |
| OPS-UX complete journey | `tests/e2e/pilot-operations/journey.spec.ts` — `OPS-UX the administrator reads the measured gate with the command of every check`, `OPS-UX the rehearsal runs and shows the four verdicts`, `OPS-UX the deletion request asks for the confirmation and keeps the pseudonym`, `OPS-UX the public status page shows the real state of the product` | The gate shows the measured value and the exact command of every check, the rehearsal shows its four verdicts, the deletion request stays disabled until the confirmation and answers with the pseudonym, and the public status page shows the real state of the runtime without an identity of a person |
| OPS-UX recover from failure | `tests/e2e/pilot-operations/journey.spec.ts` — `OPS-UX the empty state of the operations explains the next action`, `OPS-UX the failure state explains the failure and offers a retry`, `OPS-UX an ordinary account is refused by the operations of the pilot`, `OPS-UX a visitor without a session is told that the area belongs to the administrators` | The empty lists explain the next action, the failure of the API is explained and a retry reads the state again, and the accounts that may not read the area are told why |
| OPS-UX responsive keyboard operation | `tests/e2e/pilot-operations/journey.spec.ts` — `OPS-UX the journey has no horizontal overflow at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels`, `OPS-UX the journey works with the keyboard only`, `OPS-UX the journey works in the dark theme`; `tests/e2e/pilot-operations/accessibility.a11y.ts` — the eight runs of axe | No horizontal scrolling at the three widths, the fields and the command of the rehearsal are reached and used with the keyboard, the focus is visible, and the pages hold no violation of axe in both themes |
